=== ZERO-AUTH FINDINGS ===

1. HorizonShield MCP
   URL: https://gate.horizonshield.dev/mcp
   Method: GET/POST
   Auth: NONE (HTTP 200)
POST JSON-RPC to this URL. methods: initialize, tools/list, tools/call. Legacy initialize receives Mcp-Session-Id; DELETE with that header closes the compatibility session.
